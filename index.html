Overview

Luxury living, reimagined.

Completely renovated corner penthouse with panoramic views of Turtle Creek and Uptown, located on the Katy Trail near top-tier restaurants and entertainment.

Floor-to-ceiling double-pane windows allow abundant natural light throughout while the wraparound terrace extends the living experience outside for morning coffee or sunset gatherings above the lush greenery of Turtle Creek.

The open-concept floorplan is centered around a spacious kitchen with luxurious finishes and generous island seating. A dramatic fireplace, sculptural wine display, and elegant bar create inviting focal points for seamless entertainment.

The primary suite creates an intimate space for relaxation with hotel-inspired features like custom millwork, mood lighting, and elegant bathroom with curated finishes. Spacious secondary bedrooms, abundant storage, brand-new systems, and the latest technology reflect the quality of a true top-to-bottom transformation.

Two reserved parking spaces, dedicated storage locker, thoughtful amenities and full-service management round out this turnkey opportunity in one of Dallas’ most desirable settings.
